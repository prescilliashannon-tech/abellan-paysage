In the heart of Collioure, this small patio had long remained a passageway with no real purpose or identity. The challenge was to give it a reason to exist, without ever overcrowding it.

The existing walls, built from stone weathered by time, were kept and simply brought into the light. Climbing plants soften their mineral texture, while a collection of terracotta pots of varying sizes structures the ground like a small Mediterranean stage.

A potted olive tree, a few aromatic herbs and a discreet water feature are enough to turn this patio into a refuge, sheltered from the noise of the lower town.
