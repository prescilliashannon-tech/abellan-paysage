This Roussillon wine estate asked us to rethink its entire setting: the arrival onto the property, the gardens close to the farmhouse, and the transition into the surrounding vines.

A long avenue of olive trees was planted to guide the eye from the entrance to the farmhouse, pacing the walk like a breath between planting and architecture. Around the house, a naturalistic garden in silvery and green tones gently extends the vineyard landscape without ever competing with it.

Plant choices — grasses, rockrose, prostrate rosemary — were guided by low water needs and the ability to endure Roussillon summers without losing their generosity.
