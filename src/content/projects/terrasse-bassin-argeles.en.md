A few minutes from the beach at Argelès-sur-Mer, this villa had a sea view that the existing garden failed to make the most of. The goal was to create a direct link between the house, the terrace and the horizon.

The terrace was entirely rebuilt in light natural stone, to reflect the Mediterranean light without ever glaring. An infinity pool, set slightly back, visually extends the water to the sea line, blurring the boundary between garden and landscape.

Around it, low planting resistant to sea wind frames the space without ever blocking the view, for summer evenings that naturally stretch into the night.
