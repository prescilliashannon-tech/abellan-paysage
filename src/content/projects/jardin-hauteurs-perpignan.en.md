Set on the hills above Perpignan, this property overlooked a dry, mineral landscape that had to be tamed without being betrayed. The project grew from a simple intent: extend the house into the landscape rather than oppose it.

The garden is organised around a long mirror pool that catches the evening light and cools the main terrace. Around it, olive trees already present on the plot were kept and staged, alongside low-water Mediterranean planting: lavender, santolina, ornamental grasses.

The existing dry-stone terraces were restored and extended to structure the different levels of the land. The result is a quiet, understated garden where every element — stone, water, planting — feels as though it has always belonged there.
